Research

The lab integrates micro data, institutional detail, and quantitative macroeconomic modelling to study how policy shapes household behaviour, aggregate outcomes, and social welfare.

Research approach

Micro evidence, structural models, and policy design

The research agenda combines administrative and survey microdata with lifecycle and overlapping-generations models to evaluate taxation, transfers, pensions, health policy, and inequality.

1
Micro-level evidence
Administrative records and household data are used to discipline model behaviour and policy simulations.
2
Structural modelling
Lifecycle and overlapping-generations models quantify long-run responses to fiscal reforms.
3
Policy evaluation
Distributional, welfare, and macro effects are studied jointly rather than in isolation.

ANU research setting

The lab sits within one of Australia's leading environments for economic research and policy analysis.

CEPAR connection

Research links to the ARC Centre of Excellence in Population Ageing Research and related ageing-policy questions.

Treasury-facing engagement

The research agenda is designed to speak directly to fiscal design, modelling, and macroeconomic policy advice.

International collaboration

Projects connect with collaborators in Australia, North America, and beyond on applied macro public finance.

Research themes

Six pillars of the lab's research agenda

These themes organise the lab's work on fiscal design, household heterogeneity, and long-run policy evaluation.

Research theme

Income Taxation

Analysis of personal income tax schedules, labour supply incentives, savings responses, and the design of progressive taxation.
