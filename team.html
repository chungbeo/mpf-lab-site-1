People

The lab combines research leadership, current doctoral and research staff, alumni, and an international network of collaborators.

Leadership

Chung Tran, Leader

Associate Professor, Australian National University

A/Prof. Chung Tran is the Leader of the Macro Public Finance Lab @ ANU. His research covers macroeconomics, public economics, and development economics, with emphasis on connecting academic research to policy application. His work includes fiscal policy, retirement financing, income taxation, and quantitative public finance.
